Why We Do It

For a long time in dominant culture, the deepest divisions amongst men have been those across class, race, and culture. If we're going to lead ourselves into a better future, we're going to need every brother around the table.

In Living Leadership, our 8-month flagship program, we welcome two men coming from diverse, challenging, and historically under-represented communities who would not receive this depth of training without financial support.

These are men already leading in their communities who are ready to take the next step and become better in all facets of what they do.

This is not a hand-out. Nor an attempt to be politically correct. These men do the same work and are held to the same standard as the CEOs and software executives they sit beside.

The Ethos of Living Leadership

Over the course of this 8-month program, men train their thinking, their emotions, their relationships, and their nervous system to be able to take effective action on behalf of their purpose.

There are three components to the journey:

01

Capacities

We grow the physical, emotional, mental, and spiritual skills each man needs to live his purpose. We develop the abilities to stay steady under pressure, see situations clearly, and stay in hard conversations and moments instead of leaving them.

02

Initiations

We face what holds us back and what we thought we couldn't do. In meeting this challenge face-to-face, we step deeper into serving what we're responsible for in our world.

03

Collaboration

We have 15 men at our side holding us accountable, confronting us, uplifting us, and walking the path towards an integrated success that makes a better world.

The Structure of Living Leadership

Online + in-person consistency and challenge that changes how you live:

01

Leadership Sessions

1st and 3rd Wednesday · Evenings Online

These 2.5-hour sessions weave Council, Curriculum, and Coaching. They will likely become some of your favorite moments of the month. Every session is recorded.

02

Two 5-Day In-Person Summits

October 22–26 · March 11–15 · Black Mountain, NC

You gather with your brothers at an off-grid retreat sanctuary in the Appalachian Mountains. Supported by star-lit skies, feasts of delicious, seasonal foods, and swims in spring-fed creeks, you make inevitable what was impossible on your own. You will remember and reference what you experience in these days for the next decade.

03

Homework

Released Thursdays after each Leadership Session

Homework supports you in taking the clarity from our work together into your everyday life through practices and rituals—some of which will stay with you the rest of your life. On average, 10 minutes a day is adequate for you to squeeze the juice from these invitations.

Carpenters. Software executives. Artists. Formerly incarcerated brothers now dedicated to serving justice-involved youth. Wealth managers. Musicians. Blue collar business owners. Entrepreneurs. Meet Dom

Dominic

01 / 06

When I found Heart of Men, I was a completely different man than I am today. My entire life I was exposed to the darker side of humanity. Before I knew what violence or abuse was, it was being imprinted on my body and nervous system. By 11 years old, I found marijuana and alcohol and the freedom from oversight that only addict parents can give a little kid.

02 / 06

I spent the next decades slowly becoming a mirror of the parents I resented in so many ways, including a 15 year battle with opiate addiction. Throughout my 20's I was in and out of prison—spending the final 6 in my longest stretch and being shot by the police four times during the arrest.

03 / 06

The world I left was not the one I returned to and I shortly found myself in a similar place, on drugs, losing the people and progress I had been working tirelessly towards, thinking of the ways I could just give up and exit this world. I felt alone.

04 / 06

As I was working my way from this place; intensive therapy, yoga, Reiki, meditation, breath work, anything I could get my hands on to figure out WHY I was the way I was—I was introduced to Heart of Men. Thomas has a connection to this work so palpable you can feel it. The deep thought, the love invested, and time devoted to every activity, question, workshop, ceremony, sentence spoken is something that really stands out for me.

05 / 06

More than providing a program to follow, Heart of Men wants to see you heal. The friendships I've developed with these brothers who took the journey with me I'll cherish for my lifetime. Through this work I've found a home in my body, safety in loving and being loved by a healthy masculine connection I've never witnessed, the release of these traumas and dark clouds I thought would follow me forever.

06 / 06

If you're ready for a lasting change, ready to get to the root of the reasons you've found yourself who and where you are, looking for a true journey of self transformation you found the right place. Take the leap, you'll thank yourself later.

Meet Lature

Lature Van Duren

Lature spent eight years in prison after getting caught in a drug operation that his estranged and absent father roped him into. After coming home, he joined Heart of Men as a Full Circle Fellow. Today he leads physical and mental wellness programs at a high-security juvenile detention center in Brooklyn, and he's building the first program he'll lead through Heart of Men.

"As a result of participating in Heart of Men, what has deepened is my commitment to myself, to my relationships, and to my purpose. What's different now is that there's a clear path. In my marriage, there is a closeness with my wife that I know is a direct result of this work. We still have challenging conversations, but what has changed is how we have them. I know how to show up in those moments now and I attribute that directly to what I've learned and practiced inside this circle.

Through this program, I've discovered that what I'm here to do — what genuinely lights me up — is working with men and young men. And I'm not just feeling it — I'm actually stepping into it.

I feel equipped now. I feel resourced. I have what I need to continue growing into the man I know I am — for my family, for myself, and for our future together."
