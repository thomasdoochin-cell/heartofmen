Meet Aziz

2026–2027 Full Circle Fund Fellow

Aziz joined his first Heart of Men program in 2024 in a period of a lot of instability. Since then, Aziz has entered school, found stable work, and a long-term living situation in Asheville, NC.

Aziz called me a few days before our leadership program started this week and said,

“
Thomas, I’m finally ready for the level of consistency, follow-through, and action that Living Leadership asks. Is there still a spot left?

There was no doubt in me I wanted him in; Aziz has enormous gifts and has had quite the challenging upbringing.

In Aziz’s Words

I was born in Seattle before my mom moved us to Atlanta. Her belief was there was a glass ceiling there: dealing with crabs in a barrel in her close circles and racism outside her circles. She left her family and my father. He was serving a 10-year prison sentence and while there, he was beaten and had an aneurysm. He survived but had to learn how to walk and talk and had immense health problems until he died when I was 13. I only saw him a couple of times.

We ended up in Orlando with my grandparents—it was a lofty life in the hood. When we couldn’t stay there any longer, mom sent me and my little brother to live with other people while she worked to afford a home but housing was always a struggle. She was just a single mom with two kids. So basically up until my graduating year we lived in really weird housing situations. And finances didn’t ever get easier for me after graduating high school.

Now I’m 27 in Asheville, NC putting the pieces together, with stable housing, all of my acquired knowledge, and the heart that I started with to finish the job before it’s all said and done and do my part in holding the flame for my family!
